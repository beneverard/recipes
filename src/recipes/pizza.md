---
title: Pizza
tags:
  - Meals
servings: 3
ingredients:
  - 600g strong white flour
  - 360g lukewarm water (60% hydration)
  - 4g fast action yeast
  - 12g salt
  - 12g olive oil
  - 150g pizza sauce
---
### Making the dough

1. Add flour, yeast and water and mix on low for 2-3 minutes, until there's no dry flour
2. Cover and rest for 20 minutes
3. Add the salt and olive oil
4. Knead in the mixer on low/medium-low for 6-8 minutes
5. Proof for 60 minutes, until doubled in size
6. Split the dough into balls without kneading (~328g each)
7. Cover and leave the balls for ~25 minutes

### Assembly

1. Pre-heat the oven to max with pizza stones
2. Dust worktop with fine semolina and stretch into a large thin pizza
3. Dry out wet ingredients ahead of time, such as peppers and mozeralla
