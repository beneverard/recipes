---
title: Quesadillas
image: ""
servings: 4
tags:
  - Snacks
ingredients:
  - 4 tortilla wraps
  - 2 tins three bean salad in water (drained, rinsed and mashed)
  - 200g cheddar cheese (grated)
  - 2 tsp garlic
  - 2 tsp smoked paprika
---
1. Mix everything in a bowl
2. Evenly share mix for each tortilla wrap
3. Fold each tortilla in half and cook in a sandwich press
